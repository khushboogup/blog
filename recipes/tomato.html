Tomato Recipe

Ingredients

  1. Garlic
  2. Ginger
  3. Onion
  4. Tomato
  5. Dry chilli
  6. Spices:- Salt, pepper and coriander powder
  7. Mustard oil
  8. Peanut and sesame seed

Direction

  1. Take a pan, put some oil
  2. Chop everything
  3. Put everything in pan no need to stir
  4. Cover it for 10 min
  5. After 10 min, add spices and stir it for mixing
  6. Turn off the gas, let it sit until it cool down
  7. Take mixer jar put everything in it
  8. Add some peanut and sesame seed
  9. Grind it finely
  10. Afterward take pan put paste in it
  11. Add some water cook it for 5 min
  12. After server it and enjoy
