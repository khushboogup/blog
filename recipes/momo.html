Momo Recipe

Ingredients

  1. Wheat Flour
  2. Paneer
  3. Ginger and Garlic
  4. Onion and tomato
  5. Green Chillies
  6. Coriander Powder
  7. Salt
  8. Red chilli Powder
  9. Black pepper Powder
  10. Mustard oil
  11. Turmeric powder

Direction

  1. Gather all ingredient
  2. Make wheat flour dough and keep it aside

  3. Filling for momo
    • Grind garlic and ginger
    • Finely Chop onion and tomato
    • Chop some green chilli
    • Turn on gas on medium flame
    • Take pan, put some mustard oil
    • Add garlic-ginger paste in it, cook until it get brown
    • Then add onion cook for minute then add tomato cook for a minute
    • Add spice:- salt, pepper, coriander powder and turmeric powder
    • Mix well, then take out from pan put aside cool it down
  4. Now prepare momo
    • Take a dough, make some small ball of dough
    • Roll a ball in circular wrapper
    • Take a filling put in centre of circular wrapper
    • Start pleating of momo
  5. Now take steamer
    • Brush little oil on steamer plate
    • Place your momo and steam it for 10-15 min
