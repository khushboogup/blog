Thecha Recipe

Ingredients

  • Garlic
  • Green chilli
  • Peanut
  • Coriander leaves
  • Lemon

Direction

  • Take a pan
  • Put some ghee
  • Put Garlic, green chillies, peanut, roast it for few minutes
  • Take ohkli
  • Put everything in it, crush it till it get crushed
  • Add some coriander leaves crush it
  • Add some lemon juice and salt
Take out in some bowl and enjoy
