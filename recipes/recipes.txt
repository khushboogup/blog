Momos

Ingredient
1. Wheat Flour
2. Paneer
3. Ginger and Garlic
4. Onion and tomato
5. Green Chillies
6. Coriander Powder
7. Salt
8. Red chilli Powder
9. Black pepper Powder
10. Mustard oil
11. Turmeric powder

Direction
1. Gather all ingredient.
2. Make wheat flour dough and keep it aside
3. Filling for momo
   1. Grind garlic and ginger
   2. Finely Chop onion and tomato
   3. Chop some green chilli
4. Turn on gas on medium flame
5. Take pan, put some mustard oil
6. Add garlic-ginger paste in it, cook until it get brown
7. Then add onion cook for minute then add tomato cook for a minute
8. Add spice:- salt, pepper, coriander powder and turmeric powder.
9. Mix well, then take out from pan put aside cool it down
10. Take a dough 

Now prepare momo

1. Take a dough, make some small ball of dough
2. Roll a ball in circular wrapper
3. Take a filling put in centre of circular wrapper.
4. Start pleating of momo


Now take steamer

1. Brush little oil on steamer plate
2. Place your momo and steam it for 10-15 min

And your momo is ready









Tomato Soup

1. Garlic
2. Ginger
3. Onion
4. Tomato
5. Dry chilli
6. Spices:- Salt, pepper and coriander powder
7. Mustard oil
8. Peanut and sesame seed

Direction 
Take a pan, put some oil
Chop everything 
Put everything in pan no need to stir
Cover it for 10 min
After 10 min, add spices and stir it for mixing
Turn off the gas, let it sit until it cool down
Take mixer jar put everything in it 
Add some peanut and sesame seed
Grind it finely
Afterward take pan put paste in it 
Add some water cook it for 5 min
After server it and enjoy
